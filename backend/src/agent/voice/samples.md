## Grid 110 accelerator (youtube.com/shorts/pzmxp1JqlgU)
If you're an Angeleno looking for a free accelerator, I would recommend the
FOUND LA Grid 110 community business accelerator. I just graduated after two
months. We met every single Wednesday for two hours on Zoom. I had one-on-one
mentorship and a whole bunch of resources. But most of all, the most valuable
was finding other people in my cohort that are going through the struggles. I
can ask real questions, like payroll and making new services available for extra
income, and "what did you guys do in this scenario or that one?" Having other
mentees alongside you has been really a nice way for me to go back to basics.
And a program like this that doesn't take any equity, but provides you with free
resources and people's time to advise you, help you grow and stay in business —
that's something that Los Angeles needs, and every metropolitan city in America
could use.

## Chicken-and-egg hotel onboarding (youtube.com/shorts/hjglOEOooi8)
I've only been up for 30 days. I just onboarded the Chancellor Hotel in San
Francisco. The only thing is I need to get SiteMinder, because that's who he uses
for channel distribution. I got an email from SiteMinder that was like, "Hey, we
can't onboard you until you get 100 hotels." I got another email from a hotel
group that has six beautiful boutique options, and they said, "Well, who else is
on your platform? We'll give you one of our hotels, but we're not going to give
you the rest until we see who else is on it that looks like us." So I have a
chicken-and-egg problem, which is a very common marketplace issue, but I have to
solve it. So my issue is hotel onboarding. How can I creatively get influencers,
travel bloggers, and even hotel consultants to onboard with my new platform? I
hear "it's a great idea." That's good. But how do I get this great idea out to
everybody that has the same known problem, unsold inventory? How do I get them to
trust me?
